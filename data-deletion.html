How to delete your data

Last updated November 2, 2026

You can delete your myonlyfoods account and everything in it at any time. This works the same whether you signed up with email, Google or Facebook.

Delete it yourself (fastest)

  1. Sign in at myonlyfoods.com.
  2. Go to Profile in the menu at the top.
  3. In the Account section, click Delete account.
  4. Confirm. If you signed in with Google or Facebook, you'll be asked to sign in with it once more to prove it's you.

This immediately and permanently deletes your profile, your recipes and photos, your meal plans and shopping lists, your suggestion preferences, any recipes you shared publicly, and your login.

Can't sign in?

Email admin@myonlyfoods.com from the email address on your account and ask us to delete your data. We'll confirm it's your account and delete everything within 30 days, then let you know when it's done.

If you signed in with Facebook

You can also remove myonlyfoods from your Facebook account in Facebook's Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites. That stops Facebook from sharing your information with us in the future. It doesn't delete what's already in your myonlyfoods account, so please also use one of the options above.

What may remain

As explained in our Privacy Policy, copies of your public recipes that other people saved to their own books stay in their books, reports you filed are kept as moderation records, and backups held by our service providers are deleted on their regular schedule.
