Privacy Policy

Last updated November 2, 2026

This policy explains what information myonlyfoods collects, how we use it, and the choices you have. The short version: we only collect what the site needs to work, we don't sell your information, and we don't show ads.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it. If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email address with us; we don't get your Google password or anything else from your Google account.
  • Profile details you choose to add: location, a short bio, favorite food and restaurant, and a profile photo.
  • Your recipes: titles, ingredients, steps, notes, photos, collections, and whether each one is private or public.
  • How you use your book: your meal plans, shopping lists, likes, and suggestions you mark "Not interested" or "Not now".
  • Reports: when you report a recipe, we store the report along with your email address so we can follow up if needed.

We don't use advertising or analytics trackers.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show you your recipe book, meal plan and shopping list.
  • To show your public recipes, and your name as the cook who shared them, to other signed-in users.
  • To suggest recipes based on the cuisines you cook and the suggestions you like or hide.
  • To review reports and keep the site safe.
  • To email you about your account, such as password resets.

We don't sell your information

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information to anyone, and we never share it with advertisers or data brokers. We only share it:

  • with the services listed below that help run the site, and only what they need to do that job;
  • when you choose to share something, such as making a recipe public;
  • if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our users or others.

If myonlyfoods ever has a new owner, this promise goes with it.

What other people can see

  • Private recipes, meal plans, shopping lists and your profile details are visible only to you.
  • Public recipes can be seen by any signed-in user, along with the name on your account. Others can save a copy to their own book.
  • Likes: other users see how many likes a public recipe has, not who liked it.
  • Reports: the cook whose recipe was reported isn't told who reported it.

Services we use

We use a few outside services to run the site. They receive only what they need:

  • Google Firebase stores accounts, recipes, photos and everything else in your book.
  • GitHub Pages hosts the website files. Like most web hosts, it may log basic visit information such as IP addresses.
  • Web3Forms delivers report notifications to us by email. When you file a report, the report details and your email address pass through it.
  • Google Fonts, Unsplash, jsDelivr and cdnjs serve the site's fonts, front-page photos and a few code libraries. Your browser connects to them directly when those load.

The recipe card scanner

When you scan a recipe card, the text is read on your own device. The photo isn't sent anywhere unless you save it as the recipe's photo.

Cookies and browser storage

The site uses your browser's storage to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising cookies.

Keeping and deleting your information

We keep your information while your account is open. You can edit or delete any recipe at any time, and you can delete your whole account from your Profile page. Deleting your account removes your profile, recipes, photos, meal plans, shopping lists and public recipes.

A few things may remain after you delete your account:

  • Copies of your public recipes that other people saved to their own books.
  • Reports you filed, which we keep as moderation records.
  • Likes you gave, which are stored only as a random account ID, not your name or email.
  • Backups kept by our service providers, which are deleted on their regular schedule.

To ask us to delete anything else, email admin@myonlyfoods.com.

Children

myonlyfoods isn't for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Security

Your data is protected by Firebase's security, and our rules make sure people can only access their own private information. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong password you don't use anywhere else.

Your choices and rights

You can see, change and delete your information in the site at any time. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights, such as asking for a copy of your data. Email admin@myonlyfoods.com and we'll help.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top. For important changes, we'll let you know on the site.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Email admin@myonlyfoods.com.
