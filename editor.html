How do I copy the text from a recipe card?

iPhone

  1. Open the Camera, or a photo of the card in Photos.
  2. Press and hold on the writing until it highlights. (In the Camera, tap the text icon first.)
  3. Tap Select All, then Copy.

Android

  1. Open a photo of the card in Google Photos, or open Google Lens.
  2. Tap Lens, then Text.
  3. Tap Select all, then Copy.

Tip: lay the card flat in good light, and get the whole card in the frame.
